\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

The guarantees are marginal over families: ProtCOVER does not certify coverage for any particular protein, and the per-family coverage of small families is dominated by binomial noise. The weighted guarantee assumes that, given its descriptors, a family's labels and scores follow the same law in calibration and deployment; our S2 splits satisfy this only approximately (finite-pool sampling), S3 not by construction, and Corollary~\ref{cor:estimated} bounds the effect of estimated weights by an unobservable distance. The 30\%/80\% clustering leaves most families as single proteins, and partial-coverage homologs can fall in different families; the stricter homology components did not change our conclusions, but deeper remote homology could still link training and test families. ClinVar labels are noisy and enriched in well-studied genes, and every protein in the benchmark has both pathogenic and benign variants, which is not true of a typical query protein. The base model is deliberately simple, and set sizes would shrink with a stronger one. Finally, the support gate did not improve selective risk here; whether support predicts error for other predictors, variant classes, or benchmarks remains open.
