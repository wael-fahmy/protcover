\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

The guarantees are marginal over families: ProtCOVER does not certify coverage for any particular protein, and the per-family coverage of small families is dominated by binomial noise. The weighted guarantee assumes that, given its descriptors, a family's labels and scores follow the same law in calibration and deployment; our S2 splits satisfy this only approximately (finite-pool sampling), S3 is not constructed to satisfy it, and Corollary~\ref{cor:estimated} bounds the effect of estimated weights by an unobservable distance. S2 shifts the very descriptors that $\hat w$ uses, which favors weighting; on the natural shift of S3, ProtCOVER only matches group-weighted conformal prediction, and on held-out embedding regions (S4), where family-level calibration alone suffices, its weights add variance without a gain. Deciding from label-free data alone when to weight is an open problem. The density ratio is transductive: it needs a batch of unlabeled target families, and a single query would require a reference panel, such as the unlabeled proteome, in their place. The 30\%/80\% clustering leaves most families as single proteins, and partial-coverage homologs can fall in different families; the stricter homology components did not change our conclusions, but deeper remote homology could still link training and test families. ClinVar labels are noisy and enriched in well-studied genes, every protein in the benchmark has both pathogenic and benign variants, and the base model's within-protein standardization and the variant-count descriptor are computed over the labeled variant set, so set sizes may differ for a typical query protein. The base model is deliberately simple, and a stronger one may give smaller sets. ProtCOVER outputs prediction sets, not the evidence strengths of ACMG/AMP; mapping sets to PP3/BP4 strengths is future work. Finally, the support gate did not improve selective risk here; whether support predicts error for other predictors, variant classes, or benchmarks remains open.
