\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Conformal prediction.}
Split conformal prediction~\cite{vovk2005algorithmic,lei2018distribution} gives marginal coverage under exchangeability; Mondrian or class-conditional versions calibrate separately within categories~\cite{vovk2012conditional,ding2023class}. Weighted conformal prediction handles covariate shift with a known or estimated likelihood ratio~\cite{tibshirani2019conformal}, with coverage loss bounded by the ratio's estimation error~\cite{lei2021conformal}; related reweightings address label shift~\cite{podkopaev2021distribution} and general departures from exchangeability~\cite{barber2023beyond}. Group-weighted conformal prediction (GWCP)~\cite{bhattacharyya2024group} sharpens these bounds when the shift acts through a finite set of known groups; we use it with descriptor bins as a baseline. In protein science, conformal methods have handled the feedback covariate shift of sequence design~\cite{fannjiang2022conformal} and given error guarantees for mining protein databases for function~\cite{boger2025functional}. Localized conformal prediction~\cite{guan2023localized} reweights calibration points by proximity to the query, and robust and multi-source methods protect against families of target distributions~\cite{cauchois2024robust,liu2024multisource}. Conditional guarantees over a class of covariate shifts follow from quantile regression with the test point imputed~\cite{gibbs2025conformal}; related methods give group-conditional coverage for overlapping groups~\cite{jung2022batch} and for groups defined jointly on covariates and labels~\cite{bairaktari2025kandinsky}, all for exchangeable points. For grouped data, Dunn et al.~\cite{dunn2023distribution} proposed pooling and subsampling methods for a new group, Lee et al.~\cite{lee2026hierarchical} proved finite-sample guarantees for the pooled construction (HCP), and later work uses a few observations from the test group~\cite{mallick2026generalized}. Duchi et al.~\cite{duchi2024predictive} study prediction in multi-environment settings and apply conditional calibration to one coverage threshold per environment, which controls the probability that a new environment is under-covered. Macro-coverage~\cite{bhattacharyya2026macro} averages coverage over classes rather than over samples, in the same spirit as our family weighting. ProtCOVER performs conditional calibration at the level of families with HCP's pooling, so that its target is the expected coverage of a new family and its constant-feature case is HCP, and it uses an estimated density ratio as a feature; our strongest baseline applies group weighting to the same family-level pooling.

\paragraph{Calibration of variant effect predictors.}
ClinGen calibrated computational scores to evidence strengths for the computational evidence codes PP3 (supporting pathogenicity) and BP4 (supporting benignity)~\cite{pejaver2022calibration}; subsequent work found that genome-wide calibration masks heterogeneous performance across genes~\cite{tejura2024calibration} and proposed gene- and domain-aware calibration~\cite{chen2026gene}, and RaCoon calibrates ESM-1b at the residue level with conditional score distributions~\cite{passi2025racoon}. These methods improve calibration of point probabilities within subgroups seen in training; they do not give distribution-free guarantees, and they condition on gene, domain or residue attributes rather than on homology support relative to the calibration data. Kermut~\cite{groth2024kermut} provides calibrated uncertainty for supervised variant effect regression on deep mutational scans. ProteinGym~\cite{notin2023proteingym} supplies the benchmark and precomputed scores we use, including those of PLM-based and alignment-based predictors~\cite{rives2021biological,frazer2021disease,laine2019gemme,notin2022trancepteve,truong2023poet} and of supervised clinical predictors, which we exclude because they are trained on ClinVar labels~\cite{landrum2018clinvar}. Selective classification~\cite{geifman2017selective} motivates our risk-coverage evaluation of abstention.
